Chapter III

13.8 billion years, abridged.

13.8 billion years ago

The Big Bang

From a point smaller than an atom, the entire universe expands — space itself stretches outward, cooling as it grows.

13.6 billion years ago

The First Stars

Hydrogen and helium ignite in the dark. The first stars are giants — short-lived, brilliant, and metal-poor.

4.6 billion years ago

The Solar System Forms

A cloud of gas and dust collapses. The Sun ignites. The planets coalesce from the debris — including a small rocky world third from the star.

3.8 billion years ago

Life Begins

In the oceans of that third planet, self-replicating molecules appear. The rest is, quite literally, history.

200,000 years ago

Homo Sapiens

A species emerges in Africa with a brain large enough to wonder — and the curiosity to look up.

1969

First Steps on the Moon

Two humans walk on another world. For one brief moment, the species is united by the sight of a bootprint in lunar dust.

Today

You, Reading This
