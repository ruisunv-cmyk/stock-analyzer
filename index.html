30 Days to Fitter

Every exercise you need, structured into a plan you can actually follow. Start where you are. Progress every week.

Every Exercise You Need

Six movement patterns. Scale each one up or down to match your level.

Push

Push-Up

Chest, shoulders, triceps, core. The foundation of upper-body strength.

Beginner: Incline / Knee Progression: Slow tempo
Push

Pike Push-Up

Shoulder-focused push variation. Hips high, head toward the floor.

Beginner: Dolphin push-up Progression: Elevated feet
Pull

Deadhang

Hang from a sturdy bar. Builds grip and back strength—the first step to a pull-up.

Beginner: 10–20 sec Goal: 30+ sec
Pull

Inverted Row

Use a sturdy table edge or low bar. Pull your chest toward the surface.

Beginner: High bar Progression: Lower bar
Legs

Bodyweight Squat

Quads, glutes, hamstrings, calves. Sit hips back and down, chest tall.

Beginner: Sit-to-stand Progression: Slow tempo / Jump
Legs

Reverse Lunge

Glutes, quads, balance. Step back, bend both knees, push through front leg.

Beginner: Hold wall Progression: Walking lunge
Legs

Glute Bridge

Glutes, hamstrings, lower back. Lie on back, drive hips up, squeeze at top.

Beginner: Two-leg Progression: Single-leg
Legs

Calf Raise

Calves. Stand tall, rise onto toes, pause, lower slowly.

Beginner: Both legs Progression: Single-leg
Core

Plank

Deep core, shoulders. Forearms down, body in a straight line from head to heels.

Beginner: Knees down Progression: Longer hold
Core

Dead Bug

Deep core stability. Lie on back, extend opposite arm and leg, return.

Beginner: 6 reps/side Progression: 8+ reps/side
Cardio

Mountain Climbers

Core and cardio. From plank, drive knees toward chest alternately.

Beginner: Slow pace Progression: Fast intervals
Cardio

Brisk Walking

The most underrated fat-loss tool. 30–40 minutes, outdoors or marching in place.

Frequency: 2–3x/week Duration: 30–40 min

Form Guide

Good form makes the exercise work. Bad form makes it hurt.

Push-Up

  • Hands slightly wider than shoulders
  • Body in a straight line — no sagging hips
  • Lower until chest nearly touches floor
  • Elbows at ~45° from body, not flared out
  • Don't let your head drop or hips pike up

Squat

  • Feet shoulder-width, toes slightly out
  • Sit hips back and down, chest tall
  • Knees track over toes
  • Go as low as comfortable — parallel is a goal, not a requirement
  • Don't let knees cave inward or heels lift

Plank

  • Elbows directly under shoulders
  • Body straight from head to heels
  • Squeeze glutes and brace core
  • Breathe steadily — don't hold your breath
  • Don't let hips sag or pike up

Reverse Lunge

  • Step back, not forward (easier on knees)
  • Both knees bend to ~90°
  • Front knee stays over front foot
  • Push through front heel to return
  • Don't let front knee drift past toes

Glute Bridge

  • Lie on back, knees bent, feet flat
  • Drive hips up by squeezing glutes
  • Body straight from shoulders to knees at top
  • Lower slowly, don't drop
  • Don't arch your lower back at the top

Dead Bug

  • Lie on back, arms up, knees bent 90°
  • Press lower back into the floor
  • Extend opposite arm and leg slowly
  • Return with control, switch sides
  • Don't let your lower back lift off the floor
