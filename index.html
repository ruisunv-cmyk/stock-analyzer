Earth — the pale blue dot

In 1990, Voyager 1 turned its camera back toward home from 6 billion kilometres away. Earth appeared as a single pixel — a mote of dust suspended in a sunbeam. Every human who ever lived, every war, every love, every hope, happened on that tiny stage.

We are the only species in 4.5 billion years of this planet's history to look up and ask what we're looking at. That may be the most remarkable thing the universe has ever done.
